Targeted Experience 

I wanted to make a clicker game based on a factory/refinery style mood. I wanted to make the game a short experience. 

I am a fan of darker colors, so I decided to go with darker shades of blue, purple, and green. This relatively follows the connotation that refineries and factories have. For music, I went with metalworking and machinery sounds to be more immersive. I went with a few different images to help convey my theme, like workbenches, oil pumps, and factory icons. 

I used an oil rig as the clicker because I believed that it fit in terms as the actual clicker the best. You get money from digging oil, so I thought it fit well. As for the generators, I thought that factories icons were the best fit for them since they are automated.
